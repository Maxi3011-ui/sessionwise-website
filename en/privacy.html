Two roles, kept apart

  • This website: Mattea GmbH is the controller (Art. 4(7) GDPR) for the website you are reading.
  • The Sessionwise application: Sessionwise is used by organisations such as sports federations, squads and clubs. The organisation that invited you is the controller for its members' data. It decides whom it invites, which questionnaires it uses and what it uses the data for. Mattea GmbH processes this data as a processor under Art. 28 GDPR, only on the organisation's instructions and under a data processing agreement. We sign this agreement with each organisation before it works with real data; none exists yet. Questions and requests about your training data are best sent to your organisation. If such a request reaches us, we forward it to the organisation and help it respond.
  • Mattea GmbH's own purposes: Mattea GmbH is itself the controller for the contract with the organisation (contact persons, invoices). In our understanding this also applies to secure operation, for example limiting sign-in attempts. The exact split is set out in the data processing agreement with the organisation.

Sections 3 to 12 describe the application as it is built for all organisations. Your organisation may provide additional information, for example about its own questionnaires.

2. This website

This website (training.mattea.pro) consists of static pages. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising tools, loads nothing from third-party servers (no external fonts, scripts, videos or maps) and contains no forms.

Hosting: The website is delivered through GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. GitHub's servers are also located outside the EU.

Server logs: When a page is requested, the server delivering it necessarily receives your IP address and the information your browser sends, such as the requested address, the browser identifier and, if sent, the referring page. According to GitHub, the IP address of everyone who visits a page on GitHub Pages is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub keeps these logs; we have no access to them, and GitHub decides how long they are kept (see GitHub's privacy statement). The purpose is delivering the website and defending against attacks; the legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the website securely and simply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Transfer to the United States: According to its own statement, GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Transfers to certified companies in the United States are covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision on this framework (Art. 45 GDPR). GitHub also refers to the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Contact by email: If you write to us, we process your address and message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where a contract is concerned, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f)). We delete the message once the request has been dealt with and no statutory retention obligation applies.

3. What data the application processes

Account
Name, email address, time of registration and of sign-ins. There is no password: you sign in with a one-time code that we send to your email address.
Membership and role
Which organisation and groups you belong to, your role (administrator, coach or athlete), start and end of membership. For invitations: the email address of the invited person, who sent the invitation, the intended role and whether it was accepted.
Training plans
Sessions and recurring sessions with type, time, duration, description and whom they are planned for; cancellations of sessions, optionally with a reason.
Session reports
Type, date, time and duration of the session, perceived exertion (0 to 10) and the load figure calculated from it, how the athlete felt (five steps), notes and the fields your organisation provides for the session type. For sessions on the water also venue, wind (strength and direction) and which side of the course paid.
Daily check-in
Sleep duration and sleep quality, how recovered you feel, whether there is muscle soreness, pain (with the body area) or illness, and optionally a short sentence. This is health data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR. Perceived exertion and feeling in session reports and answers to the organisation's questionnaires may also allow conclusions about health; we treat them with the same care.
Organisation questionnaires
Answers to questionnaires that your organisation creates itself. The organisation decides which questions they contain.
Regatta results
Event, date, rankings and results of individual races, as far as they are entered.
Data from connected sports watches in preparation
Only if you connect your watch voluntarily; see section 7.
Technical data
A change log: who created, changed or deleted an entry and when (so that errors and misuse can be traced). Sign-in attempts: email address and IP address only as a non-reversible check value (hash), to limit repeated failed attempts. Server logs of the service providers in section 8 (IP address, time, requested address). Strictly necessary cookies, see section 10.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Purposes: planning and recording training; comparing planned and completed training; following load, recovery and well-being over time so coaches can recognise overload, pain or illness early; recording regatta results; managing accounts and access rights; keeping the service secure.

Data in the application is processed by Mattea GmbH on behalf of the organisation (Art. 28 GDPR). The legal basis is determined by the organisation as controller. In our understanding the following apply:

  • for account, membership, plans, session reports and results: the membership or contract with the organisation (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or its legitimate interest in supporting athletes' training (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR);
  • for health data, i.e. the daily check-in and data from sports watches: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), for minors that of their parents or guardians (section 5). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR); processing until then remains lawful.

Mattea GmbH's own purposes: the contract with the organisation including invoicing by the number of active users per month (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); statutory retention of invoices (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR); security of the service, such as limiting sign-in attempts and the change log (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Minors

Many athletes are minors. Their health data (daily check-in, data from sports watches) is only processed if their parents or guardians have given consent. From what age the young person's own consent is required in addition or instead will be clarified before launch with real users and added here.

The organisation obtains this consent before it invites a minor or before their health data is recorded. The application does not ask for consent itself today. How consent is documented, and which template text for parents we provide for it, will be set out in the agreement with the organisation before launch. Parents or guardians can withdraw consent at any time by contacting the organisation; after that no further health data may be recorded. That agreement also sets out what happens to the data recorded until then.

6. Who sees which data

  • You see all of your own data.
  • Your coaches see plans, session reports, daily check-ins, questionnaire answers and analyses of the athletes in the groups they coach, for as long as they coach them. A coach who leaves loses access immediately. Only the athletes themselves can change session reports, daily check-ins and questionnaire answers.
  • Your organisation's administrators see the organisation's members, roles and invitations and the same training data as coaches.
  • Private questionnaires: answers to questionnaires the organisation marks as private are visible to the athlete alone, not even to administrators.
  • Never: people from other organisations see no data of your organisation. Teammates do not see your session reports, daily check-ins or answers. Analyses never mix data from different organisations.
  • Mattea GmbH staff access data only as far as necessary for operation, troubleshooting or a request from the organisation. Who at Mattea GmbH is given such access and how it is logged will be set out in the data processing agreement before launch.

7. Connected sports watches in preparation

This feature does not exist yet. This section describes how it is being built; it will be updated before launch if needed.

Voluntary
Athletes can connect their own Garmin account to Sessionwise from their profile. Nobody has to; Sessionwise works without a watch. The connection is confirmed on Garmin's pages; we never learn your Garmin password.
Which data
Recorded activities with type, start time, duration and heart rate; daily values for sleep (in particular sleep duration), resting heart rate and heart rate variability. Only values needed for the purposes below are taken over. We will define the exact fields once Garmin has approved access and list them here.
Purpose
Heart rate and duration of an activity are attached to the matching training session so you do not have to enter them by hand, and load can be calculated from heart rate. Sleep, resting heart rate and heart rate variability show a recovery trend next to training load. Nothing else.
Who sees it
You, your coaches and the administrators of your organisation (for example a federation's head coaches). Never other organisations, never teammates.
Legal basis
Your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), for minors that of their parents or guardians. Whether Mattea GmbH acts on behalf of your organisation or as a controller itself for the connection to Garmin will be decided before the feature launches and stated here.
Disconnecting and deletion
You can disconnect at any time in your profile, or in your Garmin account. After that no more data is transferred, and all data that came from the watch is deleted from Sessionwise. Information you entered yourself is kept. How long deleted data may still exist in the database provider's backups will be decided before launch and stated in section 9.
No sharing
Data from your watch is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone other than the service providers in section 8. We do not pass it to any analytics or AI provider and do not analyse it with AI.
Garmin itself
Garmin is responsible for processing in your Garmin account; Garmin's privacy policy applies to that.

Garmin is a trademark of Garmin Ltd. or its subsidiaries. Sessionwise is not a Garmin product, and there is no partnership with Garmin.

9. Retention and deletion

  • In principle: data is kept as long as your membership and account exist and your organisation uses Sessionwise.
  • Trash: deleted entries stay in the trash for 30 days and can be restored during that time. After that they are deleted permanently.
  • End of membership: when your membership ends, your access to the organisation's data ends. Your previous entries remain with the organisation until they are deleted or your account is deleted. How long data of former members is kept is decided by the organisation; the period will be set in the agreement with it.
  • Deleting your account: on request to your organisation or to us, we delete your account. Your personal data in the application is deleted and your entries in the change log are anonymised. There is one exception for coaches: their name remains on the entries they wrote for the organisation.
  • End of the contract with an organisation: the organisation is to be able to export all its data beforehand. Afterwards the organisation and all its data are deleted completely. The period for this is set in the agreement with the organisation.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in the database provider's backups until they expire; it is no longer used there. We will decide how long backups are kept when the database is set up and state it here.
  • Technical data: we will set the retention periods for sign-in attempts, the change log and invitations never accepted before launch with real users and state them here.
  • Data from sports watches: until you disconnect (section 7).
  • Invoices to organisations: according to the statutory retention periods under German commercial and tax law.

10. Cookies

This website sets no cookies. The application only sets cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG); no consent is needed for them:

  • Sign-in (names start with sb-): keep you signed in until you sign out or the session expires. A sign-in lasts for up to 180 days.
  • Selection (sel): remembers which group or athlete is currently selected. Contains only internal identifiers, no names; valid for up to 180 days.

There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.

11. No automated decisions, no advertising

Sessionwise calculates figures such as the load per session or totals in the analysis automatically. They are guidance for you and your coaches. There is no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR, no profiling for advertising, no advertising and no sale of data.

12. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The database separates the organisations' data at row level; automated tests check, for every table, that people from other organisations, people without an organisation and anonymous requests can neither read nor change data. Sign-in uses one-time codes without passwords, and failed attempts are limited.

13. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

For data in the application, please contact your organisation first; it is the controller. We support it in this. For this website and for our own purposes, contact us directly: hello@mattea.pro.

14. Right to lodge a complaint

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for Mattea GmbH is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

15. Which data is required

Name and email address are required so you can have an account and sign in. All health information and connecting a sports watch are voluntary; without them only the features concerned are unavailable.

16. Changes

We update this policy when Sessionwise changes, for example when connecting sports watches becomes available. The version on this page applies. In case of significant changes we inform the organisations that use Sessionwise.
