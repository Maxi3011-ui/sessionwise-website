Sessionstride

Training plans, session reports and analysis for sports federations, squads and clubs in competitive sport. Coaches plan, athletes report in a few steps, and both see what was planned and what was actually done.

Sessionstride is under development and not yet generally available. It starts with competitive sailing. What is described below as available has been built and is being tested. Anything marked in preparation does not exist yet.

Who it is for

  • Organisations: federations, squads and clubs that support the training of several groups. Every organisation has its own, closed area.
  • Coaches: plan for their groups and see the reports, daily check-ins and analysis of their athletes.
  • Athletes: see their plan, report on their sessions and answer a short daily check-in.
  • Organisation administrators: invite people, assign roles and add them to groups.

What Sessionstride does today

Plan

One-off and recurring sessions for groups and individual athletes, in month and week views. Athletes see what is planned for them.

Session report

After each session: type, duration, perceived exertion on a scale from 0 to 10, and how the athlete felt on a five-step scale. For sessions on the water also the venue, wind strength and direction, and which side of the course paid. A simple load figure per session is derived from duration and exertion.

Analysis: planned versus done

Planned and completed time per session type, days on the water, hours of sailing, athletic training and boat work, per athlete and per group. The figures can be downloaded as a CSV file.

Daily check-in

Three short questions a day: sleep (duration and quality), recovery, and whether there is muscle soreness, pain or illness. Usually three taps and save. Coaches see for their group who has checked in and who is not doing well.

Roles and strict separation of organisations

Three roles: administrator, coach, athlete. Access is by invitation from one's own organisation only; sign-in uses a one-time code sent by email, without a password. Each organisation's data is separated from all others: automated tests check, for every table in the database, that people from another organisation, signed-in people without an organisation and anonymous requests can neither read nor change anything. A coach who leaves loses access immediately.

In preparation

iPhone app in preparation

An app for daily use: daily check-in, rating a session, calendar. Until then the web app also works in a phone's browser.

Connected sports watches in preparation

Athletes will be able to connect their own Garmin account voluntarily. Heart rate and duration of their recorded activities, as well as sleep, resting heart rate and heart rate variability, then no longer have to be entered by hand. The connection can be removed at any time; after that no more data is transferred and the data that came from the watch is deleted. Other manufacturers may follow.

Garmin is a trademark of Garmin Ltd. or its subsidiaries. Sessionstride is not a Garmin product, and there is no partnership with Garmin.

Training load metrics in preparation

Trends of fitness, fatigue and form from daily load. With a connected watch, load is to be calculated from heart rate rather than from perceived exertion alone.

Privacy

Training data and daily check-in answers are visible only to the athlete, their coaches and the administrators of their organisation. Other organisations and teammates never see them. Questionnaires an organisation marks as private are visible to the athlete alone. Database and application are to be run in data centres in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. There is no advertising, and data is neither sold nor shared for anyone else's purposes. Details are in the privacy policy.
